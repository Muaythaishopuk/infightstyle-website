About IFS Junior Fight League

Professional event standards

We're committed to raising standards across junior Muay Thai. Clear structures, strong safety measures and professional policies that should have been in place long ago, for a better, fairer competitive environment for young fighters.

A huge stage just for juniors

Our events are hosted at one of the best venues in the UK. Once fully set up, the arena gives young athletes a professional platform to showcase their skills in front of a great crowd.

No expense spared

No expense is spared delivering safe, professional, exciting events, while we keep ticket prices affordable so young fighters get regular chances to gain experience in the ring.
